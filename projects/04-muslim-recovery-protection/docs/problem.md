# Problem

## Statement

Some adult Muslims voluntarily want technical help avoiding pornographic content on their own devices, as part of a personal recovery effort they have chosen for themselves. Off-the-shelf blockers are either easy to disable, require invasive device permissions, or are not transparent about how they work or what they capture.

## Who this is for

Consenting adult users who opt in to self-imposed content filtering on a device they own. This is not a surveillance, parental-control, or third-party-monitoring product.

## Why it matters

A trustworthy version of this tool needs to be honest about its real technical limits (what it can and cannot block, and how it can be bypassed) rather than overselling protection it cannot deliver.

## Out of scope for this document

Personal motivations, individual user history, and religious commentary are intentionally excluded from engineering documentation. The product mission above is the complete public framing.
